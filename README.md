# Abortion and Polarization

An agent-based simulation in R and Shiny of how public opinions about abortion change and polarize.

Each simulated person has an acceptance threshold: the range of circumstances (from health risk and rape to "any reason") under which they think abortion should be legal. People also differ in conviction and openness to other views. Some hold fixed views (zealots), and some are activists who make the issue more visible.

**Main question:** Does framing abortion activism around sympathetic circumstances, such as rape, increase support with less polarization than framing it around unrestricted access?

Activists campaign with either a narrow framing (rape and health cases) or a broad framing (any reason). We simulate both and test which leads to more support and more polarization.

This is an explanatory model of how opinions could change, not a prediction about real people.
